# special_terms.txt - provenance

The term lists of the KVKK art. 6 special-category scanner
(`special.rs`), compiled into the crate with `include_str!`. The file
holds `#` comment lines, `@<category> [flags]` section lines and one
lowercase Turkish term per line. It was assembled on 2026-11-24 from a
project-curated head and generated medical sections; the generator
(`med.js`), the SPARQL queries and the exported CSV files are kept by
the maintainers outside this repository, which holds Rust code and data
only. No list was taken from another PII tool.

SHA-256 of `special_terms.txt`:
`e48145eacce23614fa04387e9aab17d973cfa5c8f640d692652a5e448c246b98`
(regenerated byte-identically from the inputs below on 2026-11-24).

## Categories (primary source)

Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (Kişisel Verilerin
Korunması Kanunu), article 6(1), the closed list of special categories:
race, ethnic origin, political opinion, philosophical belief, religion,
sect or other belief, appearance and dress, membership of associations,
foundations or trade unions, health, sexual life, criminal convictions
and security measures, biometric and genetic data.

- Official text: <https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=6698&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5>
- English translation by the Authority:
  <https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6649/Personal-Data-Protection-Law>,
  retrieved 2026-11-23.

Only the list of categories was taken from the law.

## Project-curated sections (MIT OR Apache-2.0)

Every section before the `# Generated sections` line. The words were
chosen by the redactify contributors as the everyday Turkish names of the
examples that the Personal Data Protection Authority gives in:

**"Özel Nitelikli Kişisel Verilerin İşlenmesine İlişkin Rehber"**
(Guide on the Processing of Special Categories of Personal Data), KVKK,
published 26 February 2025:
<https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8184/Ozel-Nitelikli-Kisisel-Verilerin-Islenmesine-Iliskin-Rehber>,
PDF <https://kvkk.gov.tr/SharedFolderServer/CMSFiles/70f95c73-06a2-44dc-81e9-34201bdd7f5c.pdf>
(SHA-256 `3ab35fd95275a99b72f98db25919faefb6dfca546c9214616ac4e0f41e7457a0`),
retrieved 2026-11-23. Only facts (which data the Authority treats as
special) were used, no text was copied; the page numbers are noted in
the data file:

| category | guide pages | examples the guide gives |
|---|---|---|
| race, ethnic origin | 12-14 | black and yellow race, Slav, Native American, Roma, Chechen; nationality ("uyruk") is **not** special |
| political opinion | 14-16 | party membership, being apolitical, socio-political attitudes, the answer to "which party do you support?" |
| belief, religion, sect | 16-18 | Islam, Christianity, Judaism; Protestantism, Catholicism; having no religion; worship (Board decision 2023/1356) |
| appearance and dress | 18-19 | dress and outward appearance, case by case (Danıştay 12. D., E. 2021/7000, K. 2022/2247: jeans, a beard) |
| association, foundation, union | 20-21 | an employer processing an employee's union membership |
| health, sexual life | 21-23 | hospital/clinic appointments, (pre-)diagnoses, test results, prescriptions, physical therapy, the blood group; sexual activities and relations |
| criminal convictions, security measures | 23-24 | convictions in the criminal record, imprisonment, judicial fines, conditional release, security measures (TCK art. 53 ff.), withdrawal of the driving licence |
| biometric | 24-27 | fingerprint, palm, hand geometry, iris, face and retina recognition; gait, keystroke and driving style; photographs only under specific technical processing |
| genetic | 27-29 | DNA, RNA and chromosome analyses, genetic samples, tests and their results |

The record-field labels, linking words and null values in `special.rs`
are project-curated as well; the field labels were checked against the
`train` split of `newmindai/nm-kvkk-pii-6K` (Apache-2.0, revision
`0dfe459`), never against its test split.

## Generated medical sections (Wikidata, CC0 1.0)

Wikidata's structured data is dedicated to the public domain under
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (<https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing>).
Four queries were sent to the Wikidata Query Service
(<https://query.wikidata.org/sparql>, CSV results) on 2026-11-23/24:

| export | selection | SHA-256 of the CSV |
|---|---|---|
| `icd10code.csv` | items with an ICD-10 code (P494), at least 8 sitelinks, Turkish label, lowest code | `0387d98c883481550f8b9d6d60904dd6d52b0f2ff161b1013feea57fb4fb920c` |
| `a_class.csv` | items that are an instance (P31) of disease (Q12136), class of disease (Q112193867), rare disease (Q929833), infectious disease (Q18123741) or mental disorder (Q12135), at least 15 sitelinks, Turkish label, lowest ICD-10 code if any | `6d37f950e6413fd5a5c64b5c028fc0bf13f78eceef05ea1baae0be53dcf5df34` |
| `b_alias.csv` | the same classes, at least 30 sitelinks, Turkish aliases | `1fce6d64135e9113ac1f3f9ff9202f598b6b6a7a9306bf53f124a1f30dd5a0db` |
| `atccode.csv` | items with an ATC code (P267), at least 8 sitelinks, Turkish label, lowest code | `0346bbbadf0800b37bc740b7fa43549700fc16481acfdee4c00e22034c5577ee` |

Derivation (`med.js`): Turkish lowercasing; labels of letters, digits,
spaces, hyphens and apostrophes only, 4 or more characters, at most four
words, no "ATC kodu" category items;

- diseases (section `@health i`): ICD-10 chapters A-Q (diseases, mental
  disorders, congenital anomalies) and U07 (COVID-19), or no ICD-10 code
  for the disease-class items; left out: chapter R (symptoms), S-T
  (injuries), V-Z (external causes, factors), single words that the
  in-repo word evidence (`person_words.txt`, Zemberek-derived) marks as a
  frequent word, a dictionary headword or a proper noun ("kuduz", "Kore"),
  and a project exclusion list of everyday words, slurs, article titles
  and gender-identity labels (gender identity is not an art. 6 category);
- medications (section `@health im`): ATC groups outside V (various), D
  (dermatologicals), A01 (stomatological) and A11-A13 (vitamins,
  minerals, tonics); single words that are dictionary or frequent words,
  chemical compound names (acid, chloride, oxide, alcohol, ether, salt,
  phthalate, peroxide, benzoate, hydroxide, oil, amino) and a project
  exclusion list of foods, solvents, pesticides, hormones of everyday
  language and recreational drugs left out.

Counts in the assembled file: 2,943 health terms (2,000 disease and
920 medication labels generated, 23 curated), 15 genetic, 15 biometric,
10 sexual-life, 17 criminal, 37 belief, 22 ethnicity, 23 political, 15
attire and 14 membership terms. Every left-out label is
listed with its reason in `drops.json` beside the generator.
